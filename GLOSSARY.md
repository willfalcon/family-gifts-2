# Glossary

## Events

**Attendee**
A person on an event who has replied to their invitation, either going or can't go.
_Avoid_: guest (that's a role), member (that's groups)

**Invitee**
A person invited to an event who hasn't replied yet. Becomes an Attendee once they accept or decline.
_Avoid_: pending attendee, invited attendee

**Revoked invite**
An invite a host took back before the Invitee replied. Its link stops working and the person drops off the event, but the record is kept. Inviting them again reopens it.
_Avoid_: cancelled invite, deleted invite

**Cancelled event**
An event a host called off. It stays on everyone's list marked cancelled, but its invites and join link stop working. Nothing is deleted, and a host can restore it.
_Avoid_: archived event (that's personal), deleted event (that's permanent)

**Archived event**
An event one attendee moved into the Archived section of their own events list. Nobody else is affected.

## Lists

**Occasion**
What a list is for: an event, Christmas, the owner's birthday, another date, or none ("Just ideas"). Everyone attending an event can see the lists whose occasion is that event. That includes lists from Attendees who can't go. It doesn't include lists from people a host removed from the event.

**Claim**
Marking that you'll get an item from someone else's list. Several people can split an item the owner wants more than one of. The list's owner never sees claims.

**Bought**
The person who made a Claim has marked that they've got the item. Only they see it. The list's owner and anyone else splitting the item never do.
_Avoid_: purchased, done, got it

**Private idea**
A gift idea you jot down for someone else, outside their lists. Only you ever see it.
_Avoid_: note, suggestion

**Gift to get**
One person, on one occasion, who you're expected to get something for: someone going to an upcoming event with you who has a list for it, or your Match. Mom at the Christmas party and Mom on her birthday are two Gifts to get.
_Avoid_: recipient, giftee

## Secret Santa

**Match**
The person you were drawn to give a gift to in a Secret Santa. In a Secret draw nobody else, hosts included, can see anyone's Match. In a Reviewed draw, hosts can.
_Avoid_: assignment, recipient, person

**Secret draw**
A Secret Santa where everyone is told their Match as soon as names are drawn, and nobody sees who has whom, hosts included.
_Avoid_: private draw

**Reviewed draw**
A Secret Santa where hosts see every Match once names are drawn, and nobody is told until a host sends the names. A host who is in the draw also sees who's giving to them.
_Avoid_: non-private draw, public draw

**Send the names**
When a host tells everyone in a Reviewed draw their Match. Until then the Secret Santa is **Drawn, not sent**: the Matches exist, but only hosts can see them. A Secret draw sends the names as part of drawing.
_Avoid_: publish, release, notify

**Secret Santa off**
A host has said an event won't have a Secret Santa, so hosts stop being asked to set one up. Any host can turn it back on. It can only be turned off before names are drawn, and turning it off throws away any setup done so far.
_Avoid_: disabled, round (an event has at most one Secret Santa, so just say "the Secret Santa")

**Gift ready**
Someone in a Secret Santa has marked that they have their Match's gift. Everyone in the draw sees how many are Gift ready, never who. It's separate from Claims, and it resets if the draw is undone.
_Avoid_: bought, done, got their gift

## Home screen

**To-do**
Something the app suggests you do next, like making a list for an event you're going to. It stays until it's no longer true or you dismiss it. You can't tick one off by hand.
_Avoid_: task, nudge (that's something else)

**Notice**
Something that happened that you should know about, like a draw being redone. It stays until you dismiss it.
_Avoid_: notification, alert, to-do

## People

**Connection**
Someone who shares at least one group or event with you. Connections always see each other's full name, whatever the Discoverable setting is.

**Discoverable**
A person who has "Show up in search" turned on, so people who don't already share a group or event with them can find them by name or email.
