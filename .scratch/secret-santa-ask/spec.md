# Secret Santa Ask

Status: in progress

Asking your Match for ideas without them knowing it's you. Builds on .scratch/ask-for-ideas/spec.md. See **Secret Santa Ask** in GLOSSARY.md.

## Where you can ask

- On the Secret Santa page, under your Match's items, once you've revealed them. Also on the Match card in gift shopping, once shown.
- Only once names are sent, and only while your Match has no list for the event with an item in it.
- Never on the event page: a button by one person would give away who you have.
- Once the Thread exists, both places show a Message link to it instead, whether or not they've made a list since.

## The sheet

- "Ask anonymously" title, your Match's avatar and name.
- "Mom won't see your name."
- An empty message box and the suggestion chips. No pre-filled message.
- "You asked 3 days ago" when your Secret Santa Ask is still open. Sending adds to it.
- Nobody else's Asks are shown, and other people's sheets never show this one.

## The Thread

- Its own Thread, separate from the one the two of you already share. One per Match.
- Your Match sees the giver only as "Your Secret Santa", with a gift avatar. Their name, id and colour never reach your Match's app.
- The giver sees "Mom · Secret Santa".
- Two-way: your Match can reply.
- The "made a list" line works like any Ask.
- When the draw is undone, the Ask closes and the Thread can't be replied to: "This Secret Santa was drawn again." Never revealed, even after the event.

## Home screen

- Its own card for your Match, apart from the other Asks: "Your Secret Santa asked for ideas", the event, and the Thread. No Make a list button on the card, since it's most likely a specific question. The Thread still has its Make a list banner.
- Not counted in other Ask cards' askers.
